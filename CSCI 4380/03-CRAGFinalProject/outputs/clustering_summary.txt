CRAG Clustering Implementation - Summary Report
============================================================

Dataset: Amazon Unlocked Mobile Reviews
Clustering Algorithm: K-Means (k=2)
Embedding Model: sentence-transformers/paraphrase-multilingual-mpnet-base-v2

K Selection Method:
  Used Elbow Method + Silhouette Score to determine optimal k
  Optimal k for our dataset: 2
  CRAG paper used: k=4 (not optimal for all datasets!)

Results:
  Total products clustered: 34
  Total reviews clustered: 6,400
  Total clusters created: 68
  Average silhouette score: 0.1783
  Data reduction: 98.94%
  Compression ratio: 94.12x
